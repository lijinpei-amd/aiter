# gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier 

The 1st parameter is a mask for the types of instructions that may be allowed to cross the SCHED_BARRIER during scheduling.

|**Mask value**|**The types of instructions that may be allowed to cross the SCHED_BARRIER during scheduling**|
|:---|:---|
|0x0000 0000|No instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0001|ALL, non-memory, non-side-effect producing instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER, i.e. allow ALU instructions to pass.|
|0x0000 0002|VALU instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0004|SALU instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0008|MFMA/WMMA instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0010|ALL VMEM instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0020|VMEM read instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0040|VMEM write instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0080|ALL DS instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0100|ALL DS read instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|
|0x0000 0200|ALL DS write instructions may be scheduled across SCHED_BARRIER.|

### Example


```python
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier(0x0)
gl.amd.cdna4.mfma
...
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier(0x0)
```
This Gluon semantic represents that no instructions may be scheduled past this `SCHED_BARRIER`. Therefore, the two `SCHED_BARRIERs` divide the scheduling region, and the instructions between them belong to one scheduling region.


# gl.amd.cdna3.sched_group_barrier

The first parameter is a mask that determines the types of instructions that you would like to synchronize around and add to a scheduling group. The values of the mask are defined above for sched_barrier. These instructions will be selected from the bottom up starting from the sched_group_barrier's location during instruction scheduling. The second parameter is the number of matching instructions that will be associated with this sched_group_barrier. The third parameter is an identifier which is used to describe what other sched_group_barriers should be synchronized with. This compiler hint can assist LLVM in achieving more optimal instruction scheduling.

### Example


```python
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier(0x0)
gl.amd.cdna4.mfma
...
#DS_READ
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_group_barrier(0x100, 4, 0)
#MFMA
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_group_barrier(0x008, 1, 0)
gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier(0x0)
```
The code above illustrates that one scheduling region is generated by two `SCHED_BARRIERs`. Our objective is to collect 4 DS_READ instructions and 1 MFMA instruction within the index 0 `SCHED_GROUP_BARRIER` to construct a pipeline for better performance.

Please note that `gl.amd.cdna3.sched_group_barrier` shall be placed in close proximity to the second `gl.amd.cdna3.sched_barrier`, due to the fact that the search process follows a bottom-up order.
